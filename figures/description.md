Overview of the fully data-drive workflow used to identify gene- and gene pair-based predictors of Alzheimer’s disease pathology. Single-nucleus transcriptomic data from MTG and A9 brain regions were processed to generate gene fraction and co-expression matrices. Feature selection was performed across multiple cell splits (for MI-based features) and donor splits (for gene-pair features). Selected features were used to train tree-based regression models. The best predictive models for 6E10 and AT8 pathology burden were selected using within-region cross-validation and evaluated for cross-region generalization performance.
